iOS/iPadOS Settings URLs

URLs that open a specific screen in Settings. Use them with the Open URLs action in Shortcuts.

Copy a URL, add an Open URLs action to a shortcut, and paste it in. This list was pulled from the iOS 27.1 simulator in October 2026. Apple doesn't document these, and they haven't all been tested on a device, so try one before you rely on it.

About this list

  • prefs: is the older scheme most Shortcuts examples use. App-prefs: works the same way.
  • settings-navigation:// is the newer scheme and can reach deeper screens.
  • Action starts a flow, like a sheet, setup step, or sign-in, instead of just showing a page.
  • Caution opens a page that can reset, erase, or sign out. Read carefully before tapping anything there.
  • Per-app pages likely follow settings-navigation://com.apple.Settings.Apps/<bundle ID>, including third-party apps. That's unverified.
